Open RAN (O-RAN)

Open RAN breaks the traditional single-vendor base station into disaggregated, interoperable building blocks connected by open, standardized interfaces — so operators can mix vendors and add a programmable control layer (the RIC) for AI/ML-driven optimization. My multi-vendor interoperability, fronthaul and synchronization work maps directly onto the problems O-RAN exists to solve.

The disaggregated architecture

The building blocks

ElementRole
O-RUOpen Radio Unit — RF + Low-PHY. Connects to O-DU over the Open Fronthaul using the 7.2x lower-layer split (eCPRI transport).
O-DUOpen Distributed Unit — High-PHY, MAC and RLC; real-time scheduling lives here.
O-CU (CP/UP)Open Central Unit — PDCP/RRC/SDAP, split into control-plane and user-plane over the E1 interface; connects to O-DU over F1.
Near-RT RICNear-Real-Time RAN Intelligent Controller (10 ms–1 s) running xApps; controls O-DU/O-CU over E2 (e.g. traffic steering, QoS, mobility optimization).
Non-RT RICNon-Real-Time RIC (>1 s) inside the SMO, running rApps; sets policies/ML models to the Near-RT RIC over A1.
SMO / O-CloudService Management & Orchestration manages nodes over O1; the O-Cloud (cloud infra) is managed over O2.

How my experience maps to O-RAN

O-RAN's core promise — multi-vendor interoperability over open interfaces — is exactly the problem I work on hands-on: validating mobility and interfaces across mixed-vendor RAN, and resolving real interop faults like the SCTP source-port and SRB-IE disputes. The skills transfer directly:

  • Open Fronthaul timing ← my PTP/GPS synchronization validation (the S-plane is PTP/SyncE).
  • Fronthaul transport ← my CPRI/eCPRI and backhaul/transport work.
  • E2/RIC optimization ← my KPI-driven RF optimization (xApps automate exactly these mobility/QoS/load decisions).
  • Interface validation & interop ← my Xn/NG/F1-style signalling analysis and multi-vendor issue resolution.

Presented honestly as architecture knowledge plus directly-transferable hands-on skills — not a claim of a commercial O-RAN deployment.

Why it matters & what's hard

Benefits

  • Vendor diversity / no lock-in
  • RIC-driven, programmable optimization (traffic steering, energy saving, QoS via xApps/rApps)
  • Cloud-native scaling on the O-Cloud

Challenges (validation focus)

  • Multi-vendor integration & interoperability testing
  • Open Fronthaul timing/sync accuracy (PTP/SyncE)
  • Larger security surface across open interfaces
  • End-to-end performance across disaggregated nodes
