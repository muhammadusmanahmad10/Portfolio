Massive MIMO & Advanced Radio

Modern 5G capacity comes from the radio: Massive MIMO and beamforming focus energy at each user, Carrier Aggregation stacks bandwidth, UL/DL decoupling rebalances uplink and downlink across bands, and RedCap trims the stack for IoT devices. I integrated and validated these features across macro and indoor (DAS / LampSite / small-cell) deployments.

Massive MIMO · Beamforming 4T6S · MU-MIMO Carrier Aggregation RedCap · NR UL/DL decoupling DAS · LampSite · Small cells

Why Massive MIMO matters

A Massive-MIMO AAU (e.g. 64T64R) forms many narrow beams and serves multiple users on the same time-frequency resource (MU-MIMO) by separating them spatially — directly boosting capacity and cell-edge SINR.

Indoor & dense deployments

Beyond macro sites I implemented multi-cell, multi-RAT indoor systems — DAS (distributed antenna systems with RAN sharing), LampSite indoor small-cell platforms, and dedicated small cells / IBS for urban centres. These demand careful cell planning, neighbour management and capacity dimensioning in confined, high-density environments, and apply the same RAN-sharing principles (MORAN/MOCN) as macro layers.

Field notes & common pitfalls

  • Beamforming gains depend on channel feedback. Poor CSI / mobility can erode MU-MIMO gains.
  • UL/DL decoupling is a coverage tool, not magic. It helps where mid-band uplink is the limiter — confirm the uplink band actually improves the budget.
  • RedCap ≠ full NR. Validate the reduced capability set rather than assuming full-NR behaviour.
  • Indoor needs its own neighbour/CA plan — macro assumptions don't transfer to DAS/LampSite.
