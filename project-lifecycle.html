Project Delivery Lifecycle & Financial Tracking

Standing up a RAN site is an end-to-end program, not a single task — from low-level design and bill of quantities, through civil works, backhaul, installation and integration, to drive test and operator acceptance, ending at as-built. I've owned this lifecycle across thousands of sites, including the commercial side — tracking each project's financial status (GAP1/2/3) and presenting it to management.

Financial tracking — GAP1 / GAP2 / GAP3

Beyond engineering I tracked each project's commercial status and presented it to section managers, directors and VPs. The "GAP" framework tracks where revenue sits in the delivery pipeline:

StageMeaning
GAP1In the project/PO, but the operator has not yet released/assigned any site for this PO.
GAP2Sites assigned by the operator, but not yet as-built (work in progress / pending closeout).
GAP3As-built and complete — recognised/recognisable revenue.

I built dashboards to track site milestones and financials across these stages and prepared the per-project presentations that gave leadership a clear view of delivery and revenue progress.

Highlight — owning 5G Femto from kickoff to GA

On the current program I've handled the 5G SA Femto from the beginning through to General Availability (GA) — coordinating vendor lab-validation engineers to execute test cases per 3GPP/operator standards, verifying their results, pulling in the right teams (core, RF-piping, SIM, transport) to clear blockers, and driving the beta/conditional-GA lab exit by verifying P0 test cases, fix validations and GA readiness. This bridges hands-on RAN validation with end-to-end program ownership — the same lifecycle discipline I applied to thousands of macro sites, now applied to bringing a new product to launch.

Field notes

  • Acceptance is a gate, not a formality. PAT/MTAC/OPT sign-off is what converts integration into revenue.
  • Backhaul readiness blocks everything downstream — confirm transport before scheduling integration.
  • As-built accuracy matters commercially — GAP3 (revenue) depends on a correct as-built.
