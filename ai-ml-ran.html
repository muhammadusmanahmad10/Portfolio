AI / ML for RAN

ℹ️This is a knowledge page covering where AI/ML is entering 5G RAN — the 3GPP work, the O-RAN RIC pattern, and the validation implications. Listed separately from hands-on experience.

AI and machine learning are moving from research papers into the actual 5G stack. 3GPP Release 18 standardised AI/ML for the NR air interface (study + work item in TS 38.843), Release 19 is expanding it, and the O-RAN RIC already provides the run-time platform — xApps at the Near-RT RIC and rApps at the Non-RT RIC — to deploy ML models for traffic steering, energy saving and QoS optimization. In the core, NWDAF exposes analytics to every NF.

Where AI/ML lives in the 5G stack

NWDAF (analytics) UE (Rel-18+ supports UE-assisted AI/ML procedures)

3GPP Rel-18/19 — AI/ML for the NR air interface

3GPP studied (Rel-17) and then specified (Rel-18, TS 38.843) AI/ML for the air interface, focused on three use cases where ML clearly outperforms classic algorithms:

Use caseWhat the model does
CSI feedback enhancementUE-side encoder + gNB-side decoder compress CSI more efficiently than codebook-based PMI/CQI, raising MIMO precoding accuracy and capacity.
Beam managementPredict the best Tx/Rx beam pair from a subset of SSB/CSI-RS measurements, reducing beam-sweep overhead — especially valuable for FR2.
PositioningML-assisted positioning to improve NR-ECID/PRS-based location accuracy and time-to-fix (complements the LMF-based methods I validate on the E911 page).

Rel-19 expands the framework — model life-cycle management (training, transfer, monitoring, fallback), two-sided models, and additional use cases. The standardised Functionality Identification & Management (FIM) work tracks which model is active and how to switch/disable safely.

O-RAN RIC — where models run today

LayerWhat it doesExample apps
Non-RT RIC (in SMO)Long timescale (>1 s): trains models, sets policies, A1 guidance to Near-RT RIC.rApps: traffic forecasting, KPI-driven policy, energy-saving recommendations, slice SLA assurance.
Near-RT RICShort timescale (10 ms–1 s): consumes E2 reports, runs inference, applies control to O-DU/O-CU.xApps: traffic steering, mobility load balancing, QoS optimization, anomaly detection.

For deeper architecture detail see the Open RAN page. The RIC is what turns AI/ML for RAN from a paper into something that actually controls a live network.

NWDAF — analytics in the 5G core

The Network Data Analytics Function (NWDAF) is the core-side counterpart: it collects data from other NFs over the SBA, trains/runs models, and offers analytics (e.g. load prediction, slice load, abnormal-behaviour, UE mobility, QoS sustainability) to consumers like AMF/SMF/PCF over standard Nnwdaf services. Result: policy and slice decisions become data-driven rather than static.

What AI/ML changes for validation

  • Non-determinism becomes a design property. The same UE / load can produce different outcomes; tests must reason about distributions and confidence, not single PASS/FAIL on one run.
  • Model life-cycle becomes a test surface. Activation, switching, fallback, monitoring drift — each is a new failure mode to verify.
  • Explainability matters for safety-critical decisions (mobility, energy-saving) — operators need to know why an xApp made a call.
  • RIC and NWDAF need their own conformance & soak tests — model-induced regressions show up over time, not in a 10-minute run.
